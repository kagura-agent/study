🎓 Methodology Lesson

3-point validation is systematically biased upward for skewed answer distributions. When one answer is strongly culturally preferred (e.g., "be thorough" in engineering), small samples will randomly show balanced results. Need ≥6 runs per model (36 total) for reliable discriminability estimates.
